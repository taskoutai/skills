# Individual return preparation

Written from the preparer's side. Derived from four past returns; the home office method and the documentation threshold varied with the client's records.

These rules come from the team's past work and were reviewed by the team. Follow the preferred position. Use a fallback only when its condition holds, and say which one you used and why. Never take a red-line position. When no position fits, or two rules conflict, say so and ask instead of guessing.

Where a rule names a client, follow that client's position for that client's work instead of the preferred one. Red lines still apply.

## Red lines (never)

- Claim a home office for a space that is also used for personal purposes. (Home office deduction method)

## Escalate

- **Home office deduction method:** Escalate to the reviewing partner when the office is also used for personal purposes.

## Workflow

Do the work in this order.

1. **Home office**: Decide whether the client qualifies and which method to use. Rules: [`deductions.home_office`](activities/deductions.home_office.md)
2. **Charitable gifts**: Check each gift's documentation before deducting it. Rules: [`deductions.charitable`](activities/deductions.charitable.md)
